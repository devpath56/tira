Before we begin

The situation

You’re building a dashboard — a metrics card, a table, filters. The work is a tree: epics break into stories, stories into features, features into tasks. You work depth-first: pick a branch, go deep, backtrack when it drains.

New tasks surface mid-work. A dependency you didn’t know about. An idea mentioned in chat at 11pm. A task that turns out to be unnecessary.

Right now, all of this lives in chat — scattered messages and your own memory. Nothing records what state anything is in. Nothing proves what happened. And when you ask “what now?”, the only answer is your best recollection.

This demo walks through 9 moments where that breaks — each one as a story first, then the machine. Press n.
